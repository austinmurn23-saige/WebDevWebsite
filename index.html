Welcome to StudyFit

The goal of this website is to provide college and amateur athletes with practical, easy-to-follow nutrition plans, workouts and recovery tips designed to improve athletic performance. The site will guide users on what to eat before, during, and after workouts or games to help them reach their goals — whether bulking, cutting, or maintaining optimal performance levels. It will give users pre-built workouts and instructions for many different individual exercises. Finally, it combines many trusted resources as well as compiles helpfull information to help athletes recover and be ready to do it all again.



What Results Can YOU Expect?

Gain a better understanding of how nutrition affects performance
You will develope a much deeper understanding for how preparation, whether that be, nutritrition, recovery, or simply working out the right muscle group, affects your competitive or day to day performance.
Have controll over weight gain or weight loss
Our custom meal plans allow you to have much more controll over weight gain and weight loss. The meal/workout plans also give you a much deeper understanding into how calorie intake and exercise interact to effect your weight.
Learn how to cook simple healthy meals
Our meal prep guide will teach you how to cook quick, easy meals, you can eat on the go that still give you good calories and macros. While healthy these meals will also taste great and fill up your stomach.

About Us:


We're Julian Radossich, Michael Kobus, and Austin Murn — three college students who built StudyFit with one goal in mind: to help college and amateur athletes take control of their fitness, nutrition, and recovery.


As student-athletes ourselves, we know what it's like to juggle classes, sports, late-night studying, and everything in between. We created StudyFit to make training simpler, healthier, and more accessible for anyone trying to improve themselves. Whether you're preparing for a season, hitting the gym with friends, or just trying to feel better day-to-day.


Across our site, you'll find workout guides that break down each exercise with photos, videos, and form cues so you can lift confidently and safely. You'll also find meal prep and nutrition advice designed to keep you fueled during long classes and practices, plus recovery principles focused on stretching, hydration, sleep, and mobility to help your body stay injury-free.


Everything we put on this site is built around the same idea: College athletes deserve clear, trustworthy information that actually fits their schedule, lifestyle, and goals.
